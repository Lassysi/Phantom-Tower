Phantom Tower 3.0.0 (major update)

[Important] 3.0.0 starts a new tower run. Old run files move to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PBZTower\legacy-<old version>; records are kept and your campaign save is unaffected. If you are still inside a tower run, load the game, choose Save and Exit to return to your campaign save, then update.

New
· A hex-style rune system with Common, Rare and Legendary tiers. Pick one of three at set points during the climb; some runes work together. Discover the effects in game.
· Endless mode: keep climbing after Floor 100, with growing difficulty, growing rewards and checkpoints.
· New large arenas, with multi-boss, multi-wave fights later on.
· Accessory upgrades and new resistance stats.
· Endless mode keeps granting cycle points to keep raising your abilities.
· Reworked shop and Soul Jade economy, with a smoother buying flow.
· Reworked boss schedule: more variety early on and better placement of each boss.
· Rebalanced bosses and mobs across the whole climb, without sudden difficulty spikes.
· Records page: paging, plus this run's runes and seed (click to copy and share).

Compatibility and polish
· Works alongside common mods such as UE4SS and ReShade. If a real conflict remains, a notice lists the other mods in the game folder and the game keeps running.
· The installer and every file carry full file details, which helps avoid false antivirus alerts.
· Fixed periodic stutter during fights and improved performance in crowded fights.
· Fixed waves not completing, stalls between waves, cleared boss floors being voided, and fog gates not staying closed.
· Item pickup notices now appear together after menus close.
· Many logic and stability fixes.
